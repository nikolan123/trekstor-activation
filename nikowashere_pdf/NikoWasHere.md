# Niko Was Here

Hi. My name is Niko and I am the reason this tablet is currently working.

After the company that made this tablet went bankrupt, this tablet's activation servers were shut down too. I got my hands on one of the tablets affected and this annoyed me. So I decided to rebuild the server so all 100K+ units sold work again.

The structure of those activation servers supports putting a PDF file here, so here it is.

If that sounds interesting to you, read the full story at https://nikolan.net/posts/fixing-ipads/, preferably on a modern device. You can also open the "Niko Was Here" app on your home screen, which has a built-in reader. Feel free to leave a comment on the post or star the project if it helped you.
